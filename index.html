About me

Hi, I’m Hemant.

I love learning about a new user, I naturally think in end-to-end systems, and I adapt fast. Right now, my biggest obsession is vibing with AI.
Over the last decade I’ve designed for B2B and B2C brands in consumer, healthcare, telecom, finance, energy, and tech. I’ve worked with startups, agencies, and multi-billion-dollar companies, so I’ve learned to move at whatever speed the room needs, from scrappy zero-to-one bets to enterprise-scale rollouts.
