About

I’m a product designer who’s equally comfortable running a room full of stakeholders and pushing pixels the night before a release.

Over the last decade I’ve designed for leading B2B and B2C brands across consumer, healthcare, telecom, finance, banking, energy, and technology — from IoT startups and design agencies to Deloitte Digital, ServiceNow, and now Salesforce. The hard part is rarely the interface; it’s the ambiguity, the stakeholders, and the constraints. I like getting a room to agree on the problem before we argue about the solution, then translating that alignment into scalable, user-focused products that actually ship.

This site is intentionally short — a two-minute skim. If something sparks a question, that’s the point. The full story is best told in conversation.
