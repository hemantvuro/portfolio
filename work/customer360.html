Customer360: one workspace for teams who never had one.

A full end-to-end platform that unifies fragmented customer data into a single, trustworthy view — designed through three lenses: facilitation, vision, and release.

The three lenses

How this project is best understood.

01

Facilitation

Ran discovery workshops across 4 orgs to agree on a single definition of “the customer record.”

02

Vision

Built a north-star concept that leadership used to secure a full year of roadmap funding.

03

Release

Shipped the unified workspace in three incremental releases without breaking existing flows.

04

Scale

Patterns that held up from 10-record demos to accounts with hundreds of thousands of records.

The problem

Support agents were flying blind.

7 tabs per case

Agents stitched together billing, tickets, and history by hand — losing minutes on every interaction.

Conflicting truth

Three systems disagreed about the same customer, so nobody fully trusted any of them.

No shared language

Sales, support, and success each defined “active customer” differently, and it showed.

Approach & goals

What we set out to do.

Align first

Get four organizations to one shared model before designing a single screen.

One trustworthy view

Surface the right data at the right moment, with clear provenance and freshness.

Ship incrementally

Deliver value in slices so teams could adopt without a big-bang migration.

Context

A platform, not a page.

Customer360 wasn’t a single feature — it was a foundational surface that other teams would build on for years. That raised the stakes on every early decision: information architecture, permissions, and performance all had to be right before the pixels mattered.

Because the work is confidential, the screens here are redacted. The thinking, structure, and outcomes are real; the specifics are saved for a live walkthrough.

Process

How the work was done.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Shadowed 12 agents and mapped every source they touched to close a case.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Co-authored one shared data model in a cross-org workshop series.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Tested three IA directions with card sorts and clickable prototypes.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Ran task-based usability tests on real (masked) production data.

  5. 05

    Ship

    Released in three slices with feature flags and close eng partnership.

Before / after

From scattered to single-screen.

Before

Seven tabs, three sources of truth, and a lot of copy-paste. Agents built the customer story in their heads, every single time.

After

One workspace with a unified timeline, provenance badges, and role-aware detail — the story assembled for them.
