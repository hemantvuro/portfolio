The problem

A powerful tool people avoided.

Steep ramp

Builders needed deep product knowledge before they could assemble anything useful.

Hidden capability

Powerful features were buried, so most users touched a fraction of the tool.

Low confidence

People weren’t sure their component would behave once shipped — so they hesitated.

Approach & goals

What the vision had to achieve.

Prove the pain

Ground the vision in undeniable evidence, not opinion.

Earn buy-in

Give leadership a concrete, fundable picture of the future.

Show the leap

Demonstrate how AI changes the job from “assemble” to “direct.”
